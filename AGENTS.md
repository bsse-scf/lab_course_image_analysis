This repo contains course material for the Image Analysis module of the Lab course of the Single Cell Facility of D-BSSE at ETH Zurich.

Notes:
- The schedule is only a sketch of an outline and serves to communicate instructor intentions. It can be improved
- The course should be pedagogically sound and useful, and simple at the same time.
- In terms of example data, datasets from the BBBC (Broad Bioimage Benchmark Collection) should be used (or subsets thereof)
- if applicable, useful existing course materials should be leveraged from high quality sources
