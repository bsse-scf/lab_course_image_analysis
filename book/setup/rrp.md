# Reproducible Research Platform

The [Reproducible Research Platform (RRP)](https://rrp.ethz.ch) runs the whole
course environment (JupyterLab, Fiji and the data) on ETH servers, in
the browser. Nothing needs to be installed on the local computer.

1. [Using the RRP](rrp_using.md): log in, clone the course project and open
   JupyterLab.
2. [Accessing the RRP from outside ETH](rrp_vpn.md): connect through the ETH
   VPN when you are off campus.
